英語2級レベル 予想問題の例

問1（語彙・熟語）

Choose the correct meaning of the phrasal verb 'look after' in the sentence: Can you look after my dog while I am away?

  1. to take care of
  2. to search for
  3. to look at
  4. to ignore
正解と解説を見る

正解：to take care of

'Look after' means to take care of someone or something.

問2（文法）

Which sentence is passive voice in the present perfect tense?

  1. They have built a new bridge.
  2. A new bridge has been built.
  3. They build a new bridge.
  4. A new bridge was built.
正解と解説を見る

正解：A new bridge has been built.

Present perfect passive is formed as 'has/have been + past participle', as in 'has been built'.

問3（読解）

Tom loves reading books about history. Last week, he borrowed a book about ancient Egypt from the library. What did Tom borrow from the library?

  1. A book about sports
  2. A newspaper from yesterday
  3. A magazine about science
  4. A book about ancient Egypt
正解と解説を見る

正解：A book about ancient Egypt

Tom borrowed a book about ancient Egypt, as stated in the first sentence.

問4（ライティング知識）

Which sentence best expresses a clear opinion in an essay about the benefits of exercise?

  1. Regular exercise is essential for a healthy lifestyle.
  2. Exercise can sometimes be tiring and hard to do.
  3. Many people like to watch sports on TV.
  4. Some exercises can be done indoors or outdoors.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Regular exercise is essential for a healthy lifestyle.

Choice b clearly states a strong opinion about the importance of exercise, suitable for an opinion essay.

問5（語彙・熟語）

What is the best synonym for the word "rapid" as used in: "The company saw rapid growth last year."?

  1. quick
  2. slow
  3. weak
  4. small
正解と解説を見る

正解：quick

"Rapid" means very fast or quick, especially in the context of growth or change.

問6（文法）

Which sentence correctly compares two people?

  1. She is taller than her brother.
  2. She is more taller than her brother.
  3. She is as tall than her brother.
  4. She is tallest than her brother.
正解と解説を見る

正解：She is taller than her brother.

The comparative form 'taller than' correctly compares two people.
