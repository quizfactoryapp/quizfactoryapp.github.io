実用英語技能検定 予想問題の例

問1（筆記（語彙・読解））

Read the email and answer the question: What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce a new project
  2. To request reports
  3. To cancel the meeting
  4. To inform about a schedule change
正解と解説を見る

正解：To inform about a schedule change

The email's main message is to inform recipients about the new meeting date and time.

問2（リスニング）

Listen to the conversation and answer the question. Woman: Are you going to the library after school, Ken? Man: I wanted to, but I have soccer practice today. Could you return this book for me? Woman: Sure. Do you need to borrow anything else? Man: No, thanks. I already downloaded the article for our science homework. Question: What does Ken ask the woman to do?

  1. Help him finish his science homework.
  2. Return a book to the library.
  3. Go to soccer practice with him.
  4. Download an article for him.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Return a book to the library.

Kenは練習で図書館に行けないため、本の返却を女性に頼んでいる。記事はすでに入手済み。

問3（ライティング・スピーキング）

In a short speech, you want to give your opinion clearly. Which sentence is the best opening?

  1. There are many kinds of people in the world.
  2. I believe students should do homework every day for two reasons.
  3. Homework is something that some students do after school.
  4. Today, I will talk about many interesting things.
正解と解説を見る

正解：I believe students should do homework every day for two reasons.

意見と理由の数を最初に示す文が、英検のスピーチや作文では明確で展開しやすい。

問4（筆記（語彙・読解））

Read the table on monthly sales and answer: Which month had the highest sales?

  1. February
  2. March
  3. April
  4. January
正解と解説を見る

正解：March

March had the highest sales with 150 thousand units.

問5（リスニング）

Listen to the conversation and answer the question. Woman: Hi, Ken. Are you still coming to the movie tonight? Man: Yes, but I have to finish my homework first. What time does it start? Woman: At 7:30. Let’s meet in front of the theater at 7:10. Man: OK. I’ll take the 6:45 bus, so I should be there on time. Question: What will the man do before going to the theater?

  1. Finish his homework
  2. Call the theater
  3. Meet the woman at 6:45
  4. Buy movie tickets online
正解と解説を見る

正解：Finish his homework

男性は映画館へ行く前に宿題を終える必要があると言っている。6:45はバスの時刻で、待ち合わせ時刻ではない。

問6（筆記（語彙・読解））

Read the note and choose the correct statement: What should customers do if they want to return an item?

  1. A receipt and authorization number are needed for returns.
  2. Returns are accepted only after 30 days.
  3. Items cannot be returned at all.
  4. No contact with customer service is necessary.
正解と解説を見る

正解：A receipt and authorization number are needed for returns.

Customers must have a receipt and get a return authorization number to return items within 30 days.
