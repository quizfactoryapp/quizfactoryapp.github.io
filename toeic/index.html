ビジネス英語テスト 予想問題の例

問1（読解）

You receive an email from your manager asking you to submit the monthly report by Friday. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce a new company policy
  2. To inform about a holiday schedule
  3. To ask for the monthly report submission
  4. To request a meeting next week
正解と解説を見る

正解：To ask for the monthly report submission

The email's primary goal is to request the submission of the monthly report by a specific deadline.

問2（リスニング知識）

Listen to the audio and answer the question. What is the best response?

  1. Sure, I will send it by tomorrow.
  2. I will be on vacation next month.
  3. Tomorrow is a holiday.
  4. I don’t like reports.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Sure, I will send it by tomorrow.

The speaker requests the report by tomorrow, so agreeing to send it is the correct response.

問3（読解）

An advertisement says: “Buy one, get one free until Sunday!” What does this promotion mean?

  1. The price of one product is reduced by half
  2. You pay for two products but get one free
  3. You must buy products before next month
  4. You receive two products for the price of one
正解と解説を見る

正解：You receive two products for the price of one

The promotion offers two items for the price of one, valid until Sunday.

問4（リスニング知識）

Listen to the audio and answer the question. What does the man mean?

  1. The meeting was unsuccessful.
  2. He missed the meeting.
  3. He enjoyed the meeting.
  4. He was late for the meeting.
正解と解説を見る

正解：He enjoyed the meeting.

The man's reply indicates the meeting went well and he had a positive experience.

問5（読解）

A news article reports a new software update that improves security features. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new company hiring plan
  2. A price increase for software products
  3. Improvements in software security
  4. New marketing strategies for software
正解と解説を見る

正解：Improvements in software security

The article focuses on enhancements made to the software’s security features.

問6（リスニング知識）

Listen to the audio and answer the question. What should passengers do?

  1. Go to platform 1 immediately.
  2. Leave the station now.
  3. Wait at platform 3 for the train.
  4. Buy a ticket at platform 5.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Wait at platform 3 for the train.

Passengers should wait at platform 3 since the train to Central Station will arrive there soon.
