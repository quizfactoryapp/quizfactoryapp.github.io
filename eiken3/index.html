英語準2級レベル 予想問題の例

問1（語彙）

When you want to buy a ticket for a train, where do you usually go first?

  1. To the school office
  2. To the grocery store
  3. To the classroom
  4. To the ticket machine
正解と解説を見る

正解：To the ticket machine

A ticket machine is where people usually buy tickets for trains or buses.

問2（文法）

Which sentence correctly uses the passive voice?

  1. My mother is baking the cake.
  2. My mother bakes the cake.
  3. The cake was baked by my mother.
  4. My mother baked the cake.
正解と解説を見る

正解：The cake was baked by my mother.

Passive voice is formed by 'be' + past participle; 'was baked' shows passive voice.

問3（読解）

You see a sign at a library that says: "Please return books within two weeks to avoid late fees." What should you do?

  1. Return books within two weeks.
  2. Ask someone else to return your books.
  3. Keep books forever.
  4. Return books after one month.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Return books within two weeks.

The sign instructs to return books within two weeks to avoid fines, so returning on time is correct.

問4（会話表現）

Listen to the audio and answer the question. How do you reply politely?

  1. Thank you too.
  2. No problem.
  3. I want help.
  4. You're welcome.
正解と解説を見る

正解：You're welcome.

'You're welcome' is the polite and standard reply after someone thanks you.

問5（語彙）

Which word means a place where you can borrow books?

  1. Pharmacy
  2. Restaurant
  3. Library
  4. Museum
正解と解説を見る

正解：Library

A library is a place where books can be borrowed for reading at home.

問6（文法）

Which sentence correctly uses a gerund as the subject?

  1. Run is good for health.
  2. Running is good for health.
  3. Runs are good for health.
  4. To run is good for health.
正解と解説を見る

正解：Running is good for health.

Gerunds act as nouns; 'Running' as subject is correct.
